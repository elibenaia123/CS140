What goes in the header?

It contains the website, logo, introduction and sometimes the navigation menu.

What goes in the Nav? Can the Nav go in the header? Does it have to go in the header? Does the Nav contain links to another website?

The nav contains the navigation links that help users move between sections of a website. Yes, the nav can be placed inside the header but it doesn't need to be. Nav can include other links but it doesn't usually since nav is mainly used for navigation within your own sites.

What goes in Main?

Main has the actual or unique content you want your visitors to read or see.

What goes in the Footer?

Footer contains information that usually goes at the bottom of the webpage
