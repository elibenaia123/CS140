3. What are special entity characters?

Special entity characters is a special code that shows characters on your text which are not on your keyboard like doing copyrights(©) or hidden spaces (&nbst).

4. What are phrase tags?

Phrase tags are inline elements designed to highlight or emphasize specific words or phrases within a text block like em.

5. What is the difference between OL and UL Tags?

Ordered Lists creates an ordered numbered list indicating imporatnce while Unordered Lists creates an unordered bulleted list.
