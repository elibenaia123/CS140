Questions

What is normal flow in HTML?

Normal flow is the default way elements are arranged on a webpage.

What does float do? Why do you need to clear the float?

The float property moves an element to the left or right, allowing text and other content to wrap around it.

What do absolute, fixed, and relative do?

Absolute: Positions an element relative to its nearest positioned ancestor. Fixed: Keeps an element in the same position on the screen, even when you scroll. Relative: Moves an element from its normal position without removing it from the normal flow.
