Interests Hero

Netball is one of my favorite sports because I enjoy being active and playing with other people. My older sister used to play netball and watching her play made me interested in trying. I started playing netball when I was in 2nd grade and since then, it has become a sport that I really enjoy.

I like netball because it challenges me to improve my skills and work together with my teammates. It has taught me the importance of teamwork, communication, confidence, and not giving up when things get difficult. I also enjoy watching talented netball players because their hard work and dedication inspire me to become a better player. Overall, netball has been a part of my life since I was young hence making it very special to me.
