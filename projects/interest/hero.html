Maria Folau

Interests Hero

Maria Folau is one of the retired legendary New Zealand netball player famous for her extraordinary skill in long range shooting. I chose her as my hero because I admired her dedication, perseverance and love for netball. She has played over 150 tests for New Zealand making her the second most international player in history. By watching most of her games, she continues to inspire not only me but many other netball players through her hardwork and success on the court.

Silver Ferns Career
Mrs Folau played for NZ Silver Ferns from 2005 to 2019.
Netball World Cup
Alongside her team, they won a gold at the 2019 Netball World Cup.
Positions
She played the positions of a Goal Shooter(GS) and Goal Attack(GA).
