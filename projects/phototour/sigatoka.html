Sigatoka Sand Dunes

Sigatoka Sand Dunes National Park is a distinctive natural attraction on the island of Viti Levu, Fiji. The dunes have been shaped by wind and coastal processes over time. The area is also important for its natural environment and archaeological discoveries.
