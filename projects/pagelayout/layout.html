The Polynesian Cultural Center is located in Laie, Hawaii. Its purpose is to share the culture and spirit of Polynesia to all those that visit. It is also one of the main financial provider that supports the students stuying in Bringham Young University.

One of the best things about the Polynesian Cultural Center is that people from all over the world are able to experience six different cultures and histories. Visitors are able to learn unique dances, music, drums and even making fire with wood. All these experiences are what makes their time spent at the Polynesian Center memorable.
