Elizabeth's Final Project Proposal

Title and Topic

Beyond the Perfomance. A Day in the Life of PCC Workers or Behind the Scenes of PCC Workers.

Why I chose This Topic

I chose this topic because I feel like it would be interesting to know and understand the preparations made to help bring the aloha spirit to life at the Polynesian Cultural Center. With that, I will be focusing on behind the scenes for tour guides, dancers, luau workers, ushers and other workers who play a huge role in getting PCC ready for our guests. My goal is to highlight the sacrifices they made as well as recognizing every individuals and departments talents and contribution to this amazing center.

Contents:

  1. Home Page - Introduction, welcome message, pictures and overview of website

  2. Page 2 - Individual and department preparations including their team meetings, goals, responsibilities and rehearsals.

  3. Page 3 - Official openings, welcoming of guests and interactions with guests.

  4. Page 4 - Challenges faced, how teamwork is carried out through comunication and problem solvings.

  5. Page 5 - Memories, lesson learnt, personal growth and feedbacks/reviews.

Each page will include photographs regarding the content.
